# Suggestion backlog

Design and prose suggestions from the read-throughs that were not picked for
a round. They are not bugs (see "Process" in `docs/design-decisions.md`): the
user chooses which, if any, to do. Newest first. Each says what it would
cost in state.

## From round 15

- **Every route ends in a fight.** On the vigil path, Sedge takes the vigil
  but Nettle refuses and fights (a reduced sisters' roster), so a kind or
  neutral company also gets a mechanical climax. *One battle scene.*
- **Part 3's clock never forces a choice.** A careful company clears all
  twelve threats a day before the peak. Bring the peak forward a dawn, or
  give the hills camp a night limit. *No flags.*
- **Sending Wren home is still worse than taking her along** (both give the
  same regard and the pen is emptied either way). Give it a real edge (e.g.
  the reeve's men patrol the marsh road and trail camps are safe). *No flags.*
- **The supper that raises the valley's regard comes right after the reeve
  pays exactly its price.** Price it against something that matters. *None.*
- **The Part 1 barrow was planned as "the first crack"** (docs/trilogy-plan.md)
  and is never mentioned again. One shared line in Part 2 would pay it off.
  *None.*
- **A Stealth way past the den's pit** after a quiet entry, paying avoidance
  XP (the pit is a forced fight harder than the boss). *No flags.*
- **Past the level cap, XP could buy one end-of-chapter boon** (the
  completionist ends Part 3 with 2,565 gold unspent and nothing to earn).
  *Reopens the levelling decision.*
- **Surprise rows simulate worse than plain ones** (`chapel-caught`,
  `boss-shaken`): check how the engine places a surprised side. *Engine.*
